# 📸 Apple Photos Add-On for Instax Offline Printer

Print directly from **Apple Photos** to your **Fujifilm Instax printer** over Bluetooth Low Energy with zero cloud hops, zero sync delays, and zero hassle!

---

## ⚡ Three Ways to Print from Apple Photos

### Method 1: The 1-Click App (Fastest)
1. In **Apple Photos**, select one or more photos.
2. Click **`Print from Apple Photos.app`** in this folder (or drag it into your Mac Dock).
3. The selected photos are exported at high resolution and automatically load into **Instax Square Printer**'s queue ready to print!

### Method 2: Drag and Drop
1. Open **Instax Square Printer.app**.
2. Open **Apple Photos**.
3. Select any photo (or group of photos) and simply **drag them directly out of Apple Photos** onto the Instax Square Printer window or its Dock icon!
4. The photos are instantly imported into your queue.

### Method 3: In-App iCloud / Apple Photos Browser
1. In **Instax Square Printer.app**, click the large **`Apple Photos / iCloud...`** button (or press `Cmd + Shift + I`).
2. Your entire Photos library, Recents, Favorites, and custom Albums are displayed with date search, fast scrolling, and bulk selection.

---

## 🚀 Optional: Install to Applications
Run **`Install-Apple-Photos-Addon.command`** to automatically copy `Print from Apple Photos.app` into `/Applications` and register system file associations.
