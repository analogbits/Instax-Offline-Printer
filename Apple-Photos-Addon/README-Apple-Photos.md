# 📸 Apple Photos Add-On for Instax Offline Printer

Print directly from **Apple Photos** to your **Fujifilm Instax printer** over Bluetooth Low Energy with zero cloud hops, zero sync delays, and zero hassle!

---

## ⚡ Ways to Print from Apple Photos
 
### Method 1: Native macOS Share Menu ("Share to Instax Printer") — Most Convenient
1. In **Apple Photos**, select one or more photos.
2. Click the **Share button** `[↑]` in the top toolbar (or right-click photo $\to$ **Share** $\to$ **Instax Printer**).
3. The photos are automatically transferred directly into **Instax Printer**'s queue!
4. *(If you don't see it immediately)*: In the Share menu, click **More...** (or open **System Settings $\to$ Privacy & Security $\to$ Extensions $\to$ Sharing**), and ensure **Instax Printer** is checked **ON**.

### Method 2: Edit With
1. In **Apple Photos**, right-click any photo.
2. Select **Edit With $\to$ Instax Printer**.

### Method 3: The 1-Click Companion App
1. In **Apple Photos**, select one or more photos.
2. Click **`Print from Apple Photos.app`** in this folder (or drag it into your Mac Dock).
3. The selected photos are exported at high resolution and automatically load into **Instax Printer**'s queue ready to print!

### Method 4: Drag and Drop
1. Open **Instax Printer.app**.
2. Open **Apple Photos**.
3. Select any photo (or group of photos) and simply **drag them directly out of Apple Photos** onto the Instax Printer window or its Dock icon!
4. The photos are instantly imported into your queue.

### Method 5: In-App iCloud / Apple Photos Browser
1. In **Instax Printer.app**, click the large **`Apple Photos / iCloud...`** button (or press `Cmd + Shift + I`).
2. Your entire Photos library, Recents, Favorites, and custom Albums are displayed with date search, fast scrolling, and bulk selection.

## 🚀 Optional: Install to Applications
Run **`Install-Apple-Photos-Addon.command`** to automatically copy `Print from Apple Photos.app` into `/Applications` and register system file associations.
