# 📸 Instax Offline Printer — Adobe Lightroom Classic Plugin

Print directly from **Adobe Lightroom Classic** to your **Fujifilm Instax printer** over Bluetooth Low Energy with zero cloud hops, zero phone sync, and zero quality loss!

Supports:
- **Instax Link WIDE & Link WIDE 2™** (1260 × 840)
- **Instax SQUARE Link** (800 × 800)
- **Instax Mini Link 1 / 2 / 3 & mini Link+™** (600 × 800)

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Adobe Lightroom Classic

1. Open **Adobe Lightroom Classic**.
2. In the top menu bar, click **File $\to$ Plug-in Manager** (or press `Cmd+Option+Shift+,` on Mac).
3. In the bottom-left corner of the Plug-in Manager window, click **Add**.
4. Select the **`Instax-Printer.lrplugin`** folder in this directory and click **Add Plug-in**.
5. You will see a green light next to **Instax Offline Printer** showing **"Status: Installed and running"**.
6. Click **Done**.

---

## 🖨 Two Easy Ways to Print

### Method 1: Instant Quick Print (Fastest)
1. In Lightroom's **Library** or **Develop** module, select one or more photos.
2. In the top menu, click **File $\to$ Plug-in Extras $\to$ Print to Instax...**
3. Choose your printer format (*or leave on ⚡ Auto-Detect*) and click **Print Now**!
4. The photos are rendered in full quality sRGB and beamed directly over Bluetooth to your printer.

### Method 2: Via Export Dialog (Advanced)
1. Select your photos and click **Export...** (`Cmd+Shift+E`).
2. At the very top dropdown in the Export dialog (**Export To:**), choose **Instax Offline Printer**.
3. Choose your color mode (*instax-Rich Mode* vs *instax-Natural Mode*), framing (*Fill/Crop* vs *Fit/Letterbox*), and border color.
4. Click **Export** to print.

## ⚠️ Essential Troubleshooting Tips

1. **Turn OFF Bluetooth on your phone/tablet first!**  
   Instax printers only pair with one Bluetooth device at a time. If your phone's Bluetooth is on, the printer will be invisible to your computer.
2. **Keep the printer battery charged (>20%):**  
   Driving the physical film ejection rollers requires high motor current. If the battery is too low, the printer will flash an orange light. Charge via a dedicated wall charger.
3. **Black protective cover on fresh packs:**  
   If you just loaded a fresh pack of film, make sure the initial black plastic safety cover has been ejected.
